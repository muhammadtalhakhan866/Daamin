About Daamin

Property experience. Construction focus.

Daamin Estate & Builder is a real estate, construction and property development business based in Karachi, Pakistan. Our work spans property solutions, residential development, construction execution, renovation and project marketing.

We believe good projects begin with clear requirements, practical planning and responsible execution. We aim to build lasting relationships through communication, attention to detail and a focus on our clients' needs.

The founder's story

Sameer Khan, founder of Daamin Estate & Builder, believes lasting businesses are built on trust, strong relationships and a clear vision. With a humble, people-first approach, he serves clients with honesty and respect.

His vision reaches beyond real estate and construction: quality building, thoughtful property development and reliable service, from the first plan to the finished project, held to professional standards that give clients confidence.

He values teamwork, practical decisions and long-term relationships, and sees every project as a responsibility to deliver quality work and to protect the trust placed in the company. Grounded in humility and kindness, he leads by listening and treating people fairly.

The goal is not simply to build properties, but to build a business people trust, relationships that last, and a legacy that creates lasting value.
