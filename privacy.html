1. About this policy

This policy explains how Daamin Estate & Builder (“Daamin”, “we”, “us”), based in Karachi, Pakistan, handles personal information received through this website. It covers the home page, the inspiration gallery and the review feature. It does not cover contracts, agreements or dealings we have with you offline. See section 8.

2. What we collect

Enquiries. The enquiry form asks for your name, phone number, an optional email address, what you are interested in, an optional project, and your message. This form does not send anything to our servers. It prepares a message in WhatsApp for you to review and send yourself. Nothing reaches us until you press send in WhatsApp, and WhatsApp then handles the message under its own terms and privacy policy.

Reviews. If you write a review, we ask for your name, your WhatsApp number, the service you used, a star rating and your review text. Your name, rating, service and text may be shown on the website after our team has read the review. Your WhatsApp number is stored privately, is never displayed on the website, and is used only so that we can contact you about your review if we need to. We may correct spelling or typing mistakes before a review is shown.

Technical information. When you visit, our hosting provider automatically records standard information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability. To limit spam, a short-lived counter linked to a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address is kept when you submit a review. The password-protected staff area uses a login cookie for staff only. Visitors receive no tracking or advertising cookies from us.

What we do not ask for. Please do not send your CNIC number, bank or card details, passwords or other sensitive identity documents through the website form, a review, or an unsolicited WhatsApp message. If such details are ever needed for a transaction, they are handled through a proper agreement, not this website.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you information about our services and projects.
  • To read, check, correct and publish reviews, and to contact the reviewer when needed.
  • To keep the website secure and to prevent spam and misuse.
  • To meet legal obligations and to protect our rights if there is a dispute.

We do not use your details for automated decision-making, and we do not sell or rent them.

4. Pakistani law

We aim to handle personal information fairly and lawfully, in line with the laws of Pakistan that apply to us. These include the Constitution of Pakistan (including its protection of human dignity and privacy), the Electronic Transactions Ordinance, 2002 and the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016. If a data protection law applies to us now or in future, we will follow it and update this policy. This policy is governed by the laws of Pakistan.

5. Who it is shared with

We share information only where needed to run the website or deal with your request:

  • Hosting and storage providers that run this website and store review data on our behalf. Their servers may be located outside Pakistan.
  • WhatsApp (operated by Meta), when you send an enquiry or when we message you about a review.
  • Unsplash, from whose servers the gallery pictures load. When your browser fetches them, Unsplash can see your IP address, as with any website image.
  • Our own team and project partners, only as needed to answer your enquiry.
  • Authorities or courts, where the law requires it.

Links to other sites, such as our social media pages, take you away from this website. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

6. Keeping it, and keeping it safe

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to deal with them and for reasonable business records. Reviews stay until you ask us to remove them or we decide to take them down. Anti-spam counters expire on their own. Review data is kept in a private store, and the staff area is protected by a password. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You may ask us to tell you what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you may ask us to remove a review you wrote. Contact us using the details below, and tell us the name and WhatsApp number you used so we can find it. We will respond within a reasonable time, and we may need to confirm that the request really comes from you.

8. Property and construction matters

This policy covers the website only. When you go on to buy, sell, rent or build with us, the details we collect for that purpose, and how they are kept confidential, are governed by the agreement we make with you and by the laws that apply to property and construction in Pakistan. Project descriptions and gallery pictures on this website are general information. They are not an offer, and the inspiration pictures are not Daamin’s completed work.

9. Children, links and changes

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, please tell us and we will delete it.

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed, and the current version is always on this page.
